{% docs col__launch_code %}
First letter = launch category (O orbital, D deep space, M military missile, T test rocket, A atmospheric, S suborbital, H high-altitude sounding rocket, R re-entry test, X launch from another world, Y suborbital crewed spaceplane). The rest = the launch success fraction: S success, F fail, U unknown, E pad explosion, or a percentage for partial success (e.g. OS80). GCAT says it's reliable for categories O and D.

Source: https://planet4589.org/space/gcat/web/cat/llog.html (Batch 4).
{% enddocs %}
