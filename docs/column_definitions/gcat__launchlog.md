# Column definitions: GCAT orbital launch log

Part of the SatTrack column definitions; see `00_overview.md` for the rules and decisions.

## Batch 4: GCAT orbital launch log (`launchlog.tsv` → `stg__jsr__launchlog`)

Source: GCAT "Orbital Launch Log Column Descriptions", https://planet4589.org/space/gcat/web/cat/llog.html. Its columns "are copied from the basic GCAT files, combining data from the Launch Lists with data from the Object Catalogs". 2026-10-24 file: 31,980 rows, one per payload (every Type starts with P), 0 duplicate JCATs.

| # | GCAT col | Proposed name | Proposed definition (from source) | SatTrack note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Launch_Tag | `launch_tag` | GCAT: "an identifier for the launch. For orbital launches, this is the international (COSPAR) designation of the launch." From GCAT launch log `Launch_Tag`. E.g. `1998-067`, the ISS Zarya launch. | **Detail:** (shared block, batch 1 #3) Joins to the launch lists. |
| 2 | Launch_Date | `launch_datetime_utc` | GCAT: "the UTC Gregorian calendar date of launch", often written to the second. From GCAT launch log `Launch_Date`. E.g. `2026 Oct 23 2248:44`. | **Detail:** UTC Gregorian calendar date of the launch. The data includes time, e.g. `2026 Oct 23 2248:44`, so it's more precise than `gcat_launch_date`. Parsed with `gcat_date()`, keeping precision. |
| 3 | Piece | `cospar_id` | GCAT: "The piece designation for the object. For objects in the US SATCAT this is the COSPAR (International) designation." From GCAT launch log `Piece`. E.g. `1998-067A` (ISS Zarya). | **Detail:** (shared block, batch 1 #4) |
| 4 | Type | `sat_type` | GCAT: "At the coarsest level, we characterize the object as payload (P), rocket stage (R), component (C) or debris (D)." From GCAT launch log `SatType`. E.g. `PPTH-MU` (ISS Zarya). | **Detail:** (shared block, batch 1 #5). **Important:** when GCAT knows which satellites were on a launch but not which catalog number belongs to which, it matches them arbitrarily and sets the **9th character of SatType to `?`**. (The source page labels this column `SatType`.) 724 rows have `?` in position 9. |
| 5 | — (derived from Type) | `flag_catalog_match_uncertain` | 1 when GCAT matched this satellite to its catalog number arbitrarily, so it may be swapped with another payload on the same launch; derived from launch log `SatType` (a `?` in position 9). E.g. 1 for 674 rows. | **Detail:** TRUE when character 9 of SatType is `?`, which GCAT documents as an arbitrary match between a named satellite and the launch's catalog slots. New, derived in intermediate. Means the satellite's identity and catalog number may be swapped with another payload on the same launch. |
| 6 | Name | `gcat_name` | GCAT: "My recommended name for the object. The name is based on that used by the owner/operator." From GCAT launch log `Name`. E.g. `Zarya` (ISS module). | **Detail:** (shared block, batch 1 #6) |
| 7 | PLName | `pre_launch_name` | GCAT: "an alternative name for the object. When available, it is the name used prior to launch by the manufacturer." From GCAT launch log `PLName`. E.g. `77KM  No. 175-01` (ISS Zarya). | **Detail:** (shared block, batch 1 #7) |
| 8 | JCAT | `jcat` | GCAT: "an alphanumeric identifier (JCAT...) to uniquely tag space objects". From GCAT launch log `JCAT`. E.g. `S25544`, the ISS Zarya module. | **Detail:** (shared block, batch 1 #1) PK. |
| 9 | SatOwner | `current_owner_code` | GCAT: "the organization which currently owns the payload if it is still in space, otherwise the organization which owned the object at the time of reentry." From GCAT launch log `SatOwner`. E.g. `JSC` (ISS Zarya). | **Detail:** (shared block, batch 3 #10) The current owner of the payload if still in space, otherwise the owner at re-entry. Same meaning as currentcat Owner, so one doc block. |
| 10 | SatState | `current_state_code` | GCAT: "The country (or equivalent) associated with the payload owner." From GCAT launch log `SatState`. E.g. `US` for the ISS Zarya module; `SU`, `CN`. | **Detail:** (shared block, batch 3 #11) Country associated with the payload owner. |
| 11 | LV_Type | `lv_type` *(existing)* | GCAT: "name of the type of rocket used, as given in the LV table." From GCAT launch log `LV_Type`. E.g. `Proton-K` (ISS Zarya launch); `Falcon 9` is the most common. | **Detail:** Name of the type of rocket used, as given in the GCAT LV table. FK to launch vehicles. |
| 12 | Flight_ID | `flight_id` *(existing)* | GCAT: "an identifier for the particular launch, for example the serial number of the launch vehicle or the mission number or name assigned by the launch agency." From GCAT launch log `Flight_ID`. E.g. `395-01` (ISS Zarya launch). | **Detail:** Identifier for the particular launch, e.g. the launch vehicle serial number, or the mission number or name assigned by the launch agency. |
| 13 | Platform | `launch_platform` | GCAT: Platform is "used for mobile sites such as submarines or planes"; usually blank. From GCAT launch log `Platform`. E.g. `MLP1` (used at Kennedy, `KSC`). | **Detail:** Mobile launch platform (e.g. a submarine or plane), if any. Usually blank. See https://planet4589.org/space/gcat/web/platforms/index.html. Filled on 823 rows. |
| 14 | Launch_Site | `site_code` *(existing)* | GCAT: the launch site "tells you where the rocket went up from"; a code in GCAT's Sites file. From GCAT launch log `Launch_Site`. E.g. `GIK-5` (Baykonur) for the ISS Zarya launch; `CC` (Canaveral) is the most common. | **Detail:** Launch site the rocket went up from (see the GCAT Sites file). For air launches, the airport of the carrier plane's take-off. v3 strips a trailing `?` (1 row in this file). |
| 15 | Launch_Pad | `launch_pad` | GCAT: "the Pad gives you the specific location within the launch site." From GCAT launch log `Launch_Pad`. E.g. `LC81/23` (ISS Zarya launch); `LC40`, `SLC4E` (Falcon 9 pads). | **Detail:** Specific location within the launch site. For air launches, the runway of the carrier plane's take-off. |
| 16 | Ascent_Site | `ascent_site_code` | GCAT: "Site... of the actual rocket launch from a Platform, when different from the Launch_Site"; for air launches, the drop site. From GCAT launch log `Ascent_Site`. E.g. `WTR`, `ETR`. | **Detail:** Site of the actual rocket launch from a Platform, when different from Launch_Site. For air launches, the drop site. Filled on 147 rows. |
| 17 | Ascent_Pad | `ascent_pad` | GCAT: "location of the actual rocket launch from a Platform, when different from the Launch_Site." From GCAT launch log `Ascent_Pad`. E.g. `PAWA`, `LP3`. | **Detail:** Location of the actual rocket launch from a Platform, when different from Launch_Pad. |
| 18 | Agency | `launch_agency_code` *(existing)* | GCAT: "Organization responsible for launch services (often different from the organization owning the payload)." From GCAT launch log `Launch_Agency`. E.g. `RVSNR` (ISS Zarya launch); `SPX` (SpaceX). | **Detail:** OrgCode of the organisation responsible for launch services, often different from the payload owner. (The source page labels this column `Launch_Agency`.) Composite/uncertain, split by `explode_org_codes()`. |
| 19 | LVState | `launch_agency_country` *(existing)* | GCAT: "The country (or equivalent) associated with the launch agency." From GCAT launch log `LVState`. E.g. `RU` for the ISS Zarya launch; `US`, `CN`. | **Detail:** Country (or equivalent) associated with the launch agency. Feeds country resolution. |
| 20 | Launch_Code | `launch_code` | GCAT: the first letter "is the launch category - O for orbital... D for deep space mission"; the rest is the launch success fraction. From GCAT launch log `Launch_Code`. E.g. `OS` (orbital, success), `OF` (orbital, fail). | **Detail:** First letter = launch category (O orbital, D deep space, M military missile, T test rocket, A atmospheric, S suborbital, H high-altitude sounding rocket, R re-entry test, X launch from another world, Y suborbital crewed spaceplane). The rest = the launch success fraction: S success, F fail, U unknown, E pad explosion, or a percentage for partial success (e.g. OS80). GCAT says it's reliable for categories O and D. Data: OS 30,267; OF 897; DS 446; OF40 117; …; **category `N` (`NS` 13, `NF` 2) → NULL** in `launch_category` (undocumented). The success part is still parsed. |
| 21 | — (derived) | `launch_category` / `launch_success_code` | The two halves of the launch log's `Launch_Code`, split by SatTrack: `launch_category` is the first letter (`O` orbital, `D` deep space), `launch_success_code` the rest (`S` success, `F` fail, `F40` partial). E.g. `O` and `S` (ISS Zarya). | **Detail:** First letter / remainder of Launch_Code, as above. New, derived in intermediate. |
| 22 | LTCite | `launch_time_ref` | GCAT: "this citation is the primary reference for the existence of the launch and for the given launch time." From GCAT launch log `LTCite`. E.g. `NK9901-02` (ISS Zarya launch); `Wire`. | **Detail:** Citation that is the primary reference for the launch's existence and its launch time. See the GCAT Refs file. |
